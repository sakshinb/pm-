4. Earned Value Management (EVM)

BAC (Budget at Completion) = ₹6,49,000 cost baseline. PV is derived from the 15-week planned expenditure profile in Assignment 2. EV is calculated from each task's budget × percent complete. AC is the sum of task actual costs. All figures update when task values or reporting week change.

MeasureFormulaCurrent resultInterpretation
Cost Variance (CV)EV − AC—Positive = under budget; negative = over budget.
Schedule Variance (SV)EV − PV—Positive = ahead of planned value; negative = behind.
Estimate to Complete (ETC)EAC − AC—Estimated additional cost to finish.
Variance at Completion (VAC)BAC − EAC—Positive = forecast below BAC.
